    Background

    The Economic Research and Development Impact (ERDI) Department of the Asian Development Bank (ADB) is pleased to welcome you to the 4th Macroeconomic Analysis and Skills Training (MAST) Workshop in Jakarta, Indonesia, 27–29 October 2026.

    The MAST is part of a series of capacity-building activities organized by ERDI, and follows the successful delivery of previous editions in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia in 2023, Tokyo, Japan in 2024, and Bangkok, Thailand in 2025.

    The workshop aims to strengthen participants’ capabilities in macroeconomic and socioeconomic monitoring, analysis, and forecasting, and to support the application of analytical insights to operational and policy decision-making. Through engagement with Developing Member Country (DMC) policymakers, practitioners, and ADB staff, the workshop provides opportunities for knowledge and experience sharing and discussion of country-specific development challenges and policy priorities.

    The MAST 2026 Workshop is supported by TA 10248-REG: “Industry, Services, and the Future of Development in Asia” and TA 10790-REG: “Strengthening Macroeconomic Modeling Capabilities in DMCs”, in collaboration with the Gates Foundation and Yale Economic Growth Center.

    Objectives of the Workshop

    Upon successful completion of this course, participants will have gained the following competencies:

    • Enhanced technical knowledge in macro and socioeconomic monitoring and analysis through a combination of technical lectures and hands-on practical sessions on a range of analytical tools and methodologies, with applications to economic assessment and projections.
    • Enhanced capacity to apply analytical tools to operational and policy decision-making through practical skills to assess emerging economic challenges, generate policy-relevant insights, and inform evidence-based responses.
